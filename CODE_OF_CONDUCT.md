# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment in
all public spaces of this organisation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to public activity within this organisation's
repositories: issues, pull requests, comments, code reviews, and discussions.
It does not apply to private repositories, personal projects, or communications
outside these spaces.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful; assume good intent; give and receive constructive feedback.
- Welcome people of all backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.
- Use inclusive language; be mindful of cultural and personal differences.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, hate speech, or personal and political attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language, imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without their consent.
- Any conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional context.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, edit or remove content, and block or ban participants who
violate this Code of Conduct, at their sole discretion.

To report a violation, open a GitHub issue in the relevant repository, or
contact the repository maintainer directly via GitHub.
